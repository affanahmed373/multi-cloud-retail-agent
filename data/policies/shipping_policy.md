# Shipping Policy

## Where we ship

We ship to:

- All cities in **Germany** (including Berlin, Munich, Hamburg, Frankfurt, etc.).
- Many **EU countries**.
- Selected **international destinations** outside the EU.

At checkout, you can select your country and see available shipping options and costs.

## Delivery times

### Within Germany

- **Standard shipping**: 3–5 business days.
- **Express shipping**: 1–2 business days (available for an additional fee).

### EU and international

- Delivery times vary by country, typically **5–10 business days** for EU and **7–14 business days** for international destinations.
- Exact delivery estimates are shown at checkout based on your address.

## Shipping costs

- Shipping costs depend on:
  - Destination country.
  - Package weight and size.
  - Selected shipping method (standard vs express).

All shipping costs are clearly displayed at checkout before you pay.

## Tracking

- Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a **tracking number** via email.
- You can use this number to track your package on the courier's website.

## Delays and issues

- In case of unexpected delays (e.g., customs, holidays, extreme weather), we will inform you as soon as possible.
- If your package does not arrive within the expected timeframe, please contact us with your order number and tracking information.
