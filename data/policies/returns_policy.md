# Returns & Exchanges Policy

## Return window

- You can return items within **14 days** of delivery.
- The 14-day period starts from the day you receive your package.

## Conditions for returns

To be eligible for a return, items must be:

- In **original, unworn condition**.
- With all **tags and labels attached**.
- In the original packaging (if possible).

Items that have been worn, washed, or altered cannot be returned.

## How to return

1. Contact us via email or our contact form with:
   - Your order number.
   - The items you want to return.
   - The reason for return.
2. We will provide you with:
   - Return instructions.
   - A return address.
3. Pack the items securely and send them back.

## Refunds

- Once we receive and inspect your return, we will process your refund.
- Refunds are issued to the **original payment method**.
- It may take **5–10 business days** for the refund to appear in your account, depending on your bank.

## Exchanges

- If you need a different size or color, we can arrange an exchange.
- Exchanges are subject to stock availability.
- If the item is not available, we can offer a refund or store credit.

## Non-returnable items

- For hygiene reasons, certain items (if any) may be non-returnable. This will be clearly stated on the product page.

## Questions

If you have any questions about returns or exchanges, please contact us before sending anything back.
