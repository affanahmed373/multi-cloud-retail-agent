# Size Guide

## How to measure yourself

For the best fit, we recommend measuring yourself and comparing with our size chart.

### For tops (kurtas, shalwar kameez, shirts)

- **Chest**: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, under the arms.
- **Waist**: Measure around your natural waistline.
- **Length**: Measure from the top of the shoulder down to the desired length.

### For bottoms (shalwar, trousers)

- **Waist**: Measure around your natural waistline.
- **Hips**: Measure around the fullest part of your hips.
- **Inseam**: Measure from the crotch to the bottom of the ankle.

## Using the size guide

- Each product page has a **Size Guide** section with specific measurements.
- Compare your measurements with the chart to choose the best size.
- If you are between sizes, we recommend choosing the **larger size** for comfort.

## Size labels

We use standard size labels:

- **S** (Small)
- **M** (Medium)
- **L** (Large)
- **XL** (Extra Large)
- **XXL** (Extra Extra Large) – available on selected items.

## Custom measurements

For bridal and formal wear, we sometimes offer custom tailoring. If you need custom measurements, please contact us before ordering.

## Questions

If you are unsure about your size, feel free to contact us with your measurements and the product you are interested in. We will help you choose the right size.
