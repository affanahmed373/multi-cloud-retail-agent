# Payment Policy

## Accepted payment methods

We accept the following payment methods:

- **Credit / Debit cards**:
  - Visa
  - Mastercard
- **PayPal**
- **Bank transfer** (for pre-orders or large orders, on request).

All payments are processed securely through trusted payment providers.

## When payment is collected

- For most orders, payment is collected **at the time of purchase**.
- For bank transfers, we will provide our bank details after you place the order. Your order will be processed once the payment is received.

## Currency

- All prices are shown in **EUR (€)**.
- If you pay from outside the Eurozone, your bank may apply currency conversion fees.

## Security

- We use secure payment gateways that comply with industry standards.
- Your card details are not stored on our servers.

## Failed payments

- If a payment fails, you will see an error message at checkout.
- You can try again with the same or a different payment method.
- If problems persist, please contact us or your bank.

## Invoices

- After your payment is confirmed, you will receive an **order confirmation email** with an invoice.
- If you need a separate invoice document, please contact us with your order number.
